# Third-Party Notices

PitMetric includes selected open-source vehicle artwork in the Garage UI.

## Material Design Icons

Vehicle icons including go-kart, sports car, hatchback, car, motorbike, ATV, pickup, truck and ferry are derived from Material Design Icons by Pictogrammers.

- Project: Material Design Icons
- Source: https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/
- Repository: https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign
- License: Apache License 2.0

## Game-icons.net

The Formula and prototype vehicle silhouettes are adapted from the following icons by Skoll on Game-icons.net:

- F1 car: https://game-icons.net/1x1/skoll/f1-car.html
- Race car: https://game-icons.net/1x1/skoll/race-car.html
- Author: Skoll
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)

The SVG paths are embedded locally and use `currentColor` so they inherit PitMetric's UI styling. No third-party icon CDN is required at runtime.
